Our School

From the Nursery Class to Grade 7

Quick Step School Limited is a day school in Lusaka. Children can start in the nursery class and stay with us through to the end of Grade 7, at either our Meanwood or PHI campus. Alongside the Government Curriculum, pupils take part in sports and inter-school competitions, and extra lessons are offered.
